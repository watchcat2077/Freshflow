## 3. What I assumed that the specs do not state

- **`PriceSnapshotFeed` is the port's name.** The ruling described the port but did not name it.
  I named it so spec-01 §1.3, spec-02 FR6a and spec-03 §2 can refer to the same thing. Rename
  freely; nothing depends on the spelling.
- **A scan of an unknown SKU is `400`.** spec-04 documents `400` for `POST /api/transactions` as
  "empty line items or non-positive quantity" and defines no other failure. Rather than leave
  Phase 3 to invent a status, I wrote the unknown-SKU case into FR6a as `400`. If the team
  prefers `404` or `422`, this is a one-line change — but it must be settled before Phase 3.
- **Last-write-wins on `occurredAt` per `sku`** for `price-rule-published` consumption. The
  spec mandates `eventId` deduplication and `occurredAt` windowing generally; the per-SKU
  ordering rule is mine. It matters only from Week 6.
- **`unitCost` and `category` belong on the snapshot.** The ruling listed them as event fields.
  Carrying them into `PriceSnapshot` makes it a complete projection of the price rule and costs
  nothing; Week 5 reads only `basePrice`.
- **`effectivePrice` equals `basePrice` in Week 5**, since no `MarkdownPlan` can exist.
- **`StockBatch` invariant 4** ("`expiryAt` after the receipt timestamp") is evaluated against a
  server-assigned creation instant, because `CreateBatchRequest` carries no `receivedAt`. This
  is the pre-existing M-5 finding, unchanged and non-blocking.
