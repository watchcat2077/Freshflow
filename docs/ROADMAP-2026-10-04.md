**Steps** (small enough to act on without thinking). Owners are a proposal; change them in
the Monday meeting, not silently.

thirty-five remaining marks are the report, and the report is at zero. If only one thing
gets done this week, it is **Phase 1**: enough draft that the tutor's review on Wednesday
